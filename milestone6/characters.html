Spider-Man

Peter Parker, also known as Spider-Man, is a friendly neighborhood hero who gained powers after being bitten by a radioactive spider. He balances everyday life, school, and work with the responsibility of protecting New York City.

Batman

Batman, the Dark Knight of Gotham City, fights crime using his intelligence, detective skills, technology, and training. With no superhuman powers, he relies on strategy and fear to stop villains.

Wonder Woman

Wonder Woman, also known as Diana Prince, is a powerful Amazonian warrior princess. She defends the world with her strength, compassion, and iconic Lasso of Truth, standing for justice and peace.

Iron Man

Genius inventor Tony Stark fights threats in his advanced armored suit, constantly upgrading his technology.

Superman

The Man of Steel uses his incredible power, speed, and flight to defend Earth as one of the universe’s greatest heroes.

Black Panther

King T’Challa protects Wakanda with enhanced abilities, combat mastery, and advanced Vibranium technology.

Captain America

Steve Rogers, enhanced by the Super Soldier Serum, fights for justice with courage and an unbreakable shield.

The Flash

Barry Allen uses super speed and the power of the Speed Force to save Central City from danger.

Hulk

Bruce Banner transforms into the Hulk, a nearly unstoppable force powered by rage and strength.

Thor

The God of Thunder wields Mjolnir and defends the realms with unmatched power.

Aquaman

Arthur Curry protects both the ocean and the surface world as Atlantis' king.

Doctor Strange

The Sorcerer Supreme defends reality using magic and mystical artifacts.

Green Lantern

Hal Jordan uses a power ring fueled by willpower to create anything he imagines.

Black Widow

Natasha Romanoff is a master spy and fighter trained in espionage and combat.

Shazam

Billy Batson transforms into a magical champion with powers from ancient gods.

Wolverine

Logan is a mutant with incredible healing ability, adamantium claws, and fierce determination.

Star-Lord

Peter Quill is a cosmic adventurer leading the Guardians with charm and strategy.

Harley Quinn

Once a psychiatrist, Harley Quinn is now an unpredictable antihero with a chaotic style.

Joker

Gotham’s Clown Prince of Crime thrives on chaos, unpredictability, and psychological warfare.

Thanos

The Mad Titan seeks universal balance through destruction, wielding immense cosmic power.

Lex Luthor

A billionaire genius obsessed with defeating Superman, using influence and intellect.

Green Goblin

Norman Osborn rides his glider into chaos, fueled by ambition and a twisted persona.

Ultron

A rogue AI bent on replacing humanity with machine perfection, constantly evolving.

Darkseid

The ruler of Apokolips seeks the Anti-Life Equation to dominate all sentient beings.

Venom

A powerful symbiote who forms deadly partnerships, often torn between chaos and justice.

Magneto

A master of magnetism fighting for mutant superiority, shaped by a tragic past.

Loki

The God of Mischief uses magic, illusions, and manipulation to reshape destiny as he sees fit.

Red Skull

Captain America’s ruthless enemy, driven by ideology and a desire for absolute power.

Deathstroke

A tactical genius and enhanced assassin known for his unmatched combat skills.

Doctor Doom

Monarch of Latveria, Doom blends scientific mastery and sorcery to pursue domination.

Reverse Flash

The Flash’s greatest enemy, manipulating time and speed to rewrite Barry’s life.

Hela

Goddess of Death, wielding immense power and ruling over the dead with iron will.

Bane

A formidable foe who combines genius intellect with superhuman strength from Venom.

Kingpin

New York’s criminal mastermind, controlling crime with influence, strength, and strategy.

General Zod

A Kryptonian warrior seeking to rebuild his world through conquest and domination.

Apocalypse

One of the first mutants, believing only the strong deserve to survive his global reshaping.

Optimus Prime

The noble leader of the Autobots, valuing freedom and justice above all.

Megatron

Ruthless leader of the Decepticons, seeking ultimate power and domination.

Bumblebee

A brave and loyal Autobot scout known for his courage and strong heart.

Starscream

Decepticon air commander who constantly schemes to take Megatron’s throne.

Ironhide

A tough Autobot weapons specialist who never backs down from a fight.

Soundwave

Intelligent and loyal to Megatron, Soundwave uses communication and espionage for battle.

Grimlock

Leader of the Dinobots, strong-willed and powerful with a fierce T-Rex form.

Shockwave

A calculating Decepticon scientist who values logic and experiments above all else.

Ratchet

The Autobots’ medical officer, skilled in repairs, healing, and battlefield strategy.

Hot Rod

A brave young Autobot who eventually rises to carry the Matrix as Rodimus Prime.

Arcee

A highly skilled Autobot warrior known for her speed, precision, and determination.

Ultra Magnus

A loyal Autobot commander, powerful and disciplined, second only to Optimus Prime.

Wheeljack

The Autobots’ eccentric inventor who creates gadgets and weapons for the war effort.

Blitzwing

A dangerous Decepticon triple-changer switching between tank, jet, and robot modes.

Springer

Sentinel Prime

Once a respected Autobot leader, Sentinel Prime is a powerful warrior whose strict belief in order and survival often puts him at odds with his allies. His strength, authority, and command of advanced Cybertronian technology make him one of the most formidable Transformers.

Unicron

A colossal planet-eating entity, the ultimate cosmic threat to all Transformers.

Galvatron

A reborn Megatron empowered by Unicron, more unstable and powerful than ever.
